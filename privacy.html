Draft for review

Privacy notice

This draft explains how The Staunton Group Ltd may handle personal information received through this website. It must be reviewed and completed before public launch.

Not yet final: the group’s dedicated contact address, information-retention periods and relationships with the operating education companies still require confirmation.

Who we are

The Staunton Group Ltd is a private limited company registered in England and Wales under company number 17070776, with its registered office in Staunton-in-the-Vale, Nottinghamshire.

Information we may collect

If you contact us by email, we may receive your name, email address, the organisation you represent and any other information you choose to include in your message.

This preview website does not use analytics, advertising technology, a contact-form database or non-essential cookies.

How we use information

We may use information you provide to respond to your enquiry, keep an appropriate record of correspondence and meet legal or regulatory responsibilities. We will only use personal information when we have an appropriate lawful basis.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Where an enquiry relates to one of the group’s areas of activity, it may be shared with the relevant group business so that it can respond. The final notice will explain how information moves within the group.

We will keep information only for as long as it is reasonably required for the purpose for which it was collected, or to meet legal obligations. Specific retention periods will be confirmed before launch.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights over your personal information, including rights to access, correct, erase or restrict its use, and to object to certain processing.

You can also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk ↗.

Contact

Privacy enquiries can be sent using the contact details on the main website.
